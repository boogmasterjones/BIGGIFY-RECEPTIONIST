How do I find my link?

The best link (2 minutes):

  1. On your phone, sign in to the Google account that runs your business profile.
  2. Search Google for your business name.
  3. Your profile shows up with buttons. Tap "Ask for reviews" (it may say "Get more reviews").
  4. Tap Copy, then paste it in the box above.

No buttons? Open Google Maps, find your business, tap Share, and copy the link. That works too. It opens your profile, and people tap "Write a review".

Or use your Place ID: search your business on Google's free Place ID finder. Copy the code that starts with "ChIJ" and paste it above. I'll turn it into a direct review link.

Google's rules (keep your reviews safe)

  • Ask every customer, not just the ones you think are happy.
  • Never give anything for a review: no discount, no gift card, no prize.
  • Ask for an honest review. Don't ask for "5 stars".
  • Let them use their own phone, not yours.
  • Send one reminder at most. If someone says stop, stop.
