1. Document checklist

Put all of this in one folder, on your phone and your computer. Most networks ask for some mix of it; having it all means you can apply anywhere in an afternoon.

Licenses

  • Trade license(s) for every state, county and city you'll work in
  • Business license or registration where your area requires one
  • Any certifications you'll list (for example EPA refrigerant handling for HVAC techs)

Insurance

  • General liability certificate (COI) with each warranty company named as certificate holder, and as additional insured if they ask
  • Your liability limits written down (some ask for a minimum per occurrence)
  • Commercial auto insurance for every truck that goes to their customers
  • Workers comp certificate, or your state's exemption paperwork if you have one (ask each network whether they accept it)

Tax and identity

  • W-9 with your legal business name and EIN, matching your bank account
  • Photo ID for the owner
  • Bank details ready for direct deposit (you enter these yourself in their secure portal, never by email)

People and trucks

  • List of techs who'll go into homes, and that each can pass a background check (some also check driving records)
  • Number of trucks, what you stock, brands you service
  • Photos of lettered trucks and uniforms, if you have them

Your numbers

  • Service area as a list of zip codes you'll really drive to
  • Hours, after-hours and emergency availability
  • How fast you'll call a homeowner back, and how soon you can usually be there
  • Your true cost per call and the lowest rate you'll accept (worksheet in section 5)

Do this today: email your insurance agent for the certificates. It's usually the slowest part, and section 3 of the packet has the letter ready to send.
