Picking your main category

Your main (primary) category tells Google what you are. Google's own help names relevance as one of the three things it ranks local results on, and your main category is the biggest relevance setting you control. It's also the one we find wrong most often.

What Google's rules say

  • Be specific. Google asks for categories that are "as specific as possible, but representative of your main business."
  • Use as few as you need. Its rules say to choose the fewest categories it takes to describe your core business.
  • Only Google's list counts. You can't type your own. If it isn't offered when you start typing, it isn't a category.

Pick it in 5 steps (15 minutes)

  1. Write down the job you want most.The work that brings you the most money and the most calls. For most owners it's the trade itself: plumbing, roofing, electrical.
  2. Search like a customer.On your phone, logged out (a private window), search "[your trade] near me" and "[your main service] [your town]". Look at the map, not the ads.
  3. Read the top 3's categories.On Google Maps, the main category shows right under each business's name and stars. Write all three down.
  4. Choose the most specific one that's your whole business.If all three use "Plumber" and you're a plumber, use "Plumber". If you only do water heaters, a water heater category may fit better than the broad trade.
  5. Change it once, then leave it alone.Give it 2 to 6 weeks before you judge. Don't change your name, phone or address the same week.

Google renames and adds categories now and then. Type the first few letters in your profile's category box and pick from what it offers.

✓Do

  • Match what the top 3 near you use, if it's honestly what you are
  • Pick the trade people type into Google, in their words
  • Put your other real services in extra categories and the Services list

✕Don't

  • Use a broad one like "Contractor" when you're really a roofer
  • Make your smallest side job the main category
  • Pick categories for work you don't do. Competitors report it, and it can get edited back
  • Keep switching it every week to "test"
Seeing your competitors' extra categories

Google only shows the main category in public. Several free browser extensions made for local search can show the extra ones too. Any of them is fine for this one job. Never install one that asks you to sign in with your Google account.

The most specific category that describes your whole core business and matches what most of your best customers search, such as Plumber, HVAC contractor or Roofing contractor. Check the category shown under the top 3 businesses near you, change it once, and give it a few weeks before you judge.
