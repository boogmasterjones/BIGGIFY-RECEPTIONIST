How It Works

From "never heard of you" to booked on your calendar.

Here's every step of how Biggify gets your business found, chosen, and answered — what we handle, and the short list of what's on you.

1

Get found when they search

Someone's AC just died and they're typing "AC repair near me." The three businesses in Google's map pack get the call. Everyone else might as well not exist. Step one is getting you into that pack — and keeping you there.

Google Business ProfileLocal SEO

What we do

  • Optimize your Google Business Profile — the right categories, every service listed, hours, service areas, photos, and a description written for how people actually search.
  • Post to it every week — 4 posts a month on Growth, 10 a month on Super Growth. Google rewards active profiles; most contractors' have been dead for a year.
  • Build pages for the towns you serve — your home town on Growth, 3–5 towns on Super Growth — so you rank for "tile installer Venice," not just your business name.
  • Publish new content on a schedule — 1–2 pages a month on Growth, 4–6 on Super Growth, covering your core services and expanding to the full service line and long-tail searches.
  • Build your citations and links — the directory listings and references Google checks before it trusts you with a top spot. Foundational on Growth, actively expanded on Super Growth.
  • Grow your reviews — a request system that asks every customer automatically on Growth, or a fully managed review campaign on Super Growth.

What you do

  • Tell us your towns. Where do you actually want more work? That's where we aim.
  • Send photos from the truck. Real jobs, real trucks, real crew — Google and customers both trust them more than stock.
  • Let the review requests go out. We set them up; you just do the good work that earns the five stars.
Time on your end: a few minutes a week, mostly texting us photos.
2

Get chosen over the other two

They found you. Now they're comparing — you and two competitors, three tabs open, thirty seconds each. An outdated site with no reviews and no photos of your work loses that comparison every time. Step two is winning it.

WebsiteSocial Media

What we do

  • Build your website like a salesperson — fast, mobile-first, with your phone number one tap away on every screen. See the before & afters.
  • Put the trust signals up front — reviews, license and insurance, years in business, and real photos of your work, right where people look before they call.
  • Wire the site to your receptionist — chat and call buttons connect straight to Biggify, so a visitor at 9 PM still gets booked instead of bouncing.
  • Keep your social alive — 4 posts a month on Growth, 10 on Super Growth, across Facebook, Instagram, and your Google profile. Your job photos become before-and-after posts; your new reviews become content.
  • Keep the site current — new service pages, updated photos, fresh reviews — so it never turns back into a brochure.

What you do

  • Give feedback on the draft. You know your customers — tell us what's off, we fix it.
  • Keep sending photos. The same ones from Step 1 feed the website and the social posts. One text, three uses.
You never have to write a post, edit a page, or log into Facebook.

3

Get booked — even when you can't pick up

Everything above exists to make the phone ring. This step makes sure the ring turns into a job. You're on a ladder, under a sink, or it's Sunday night — the call still gets answered, and the appointment still lands on your calendar.

AI Receptionist

What happens on a call

  • It answers, every time. Calls you can't get to ring through to Biggify instead of voicemail. It picks up immediately, in a natural voice, using your business name, services, and hours.
  • It handles the conversation. It answers questions about what you offer and where you work, figures out what the caller needs, and checks your real calendar for open times — no hold music.
  • It books the job. If a time works, it puts the appointment on your calendar right then, while the customer is still on the phone.
  • It texts the customer. Right after, they get a short text asking for the address, the issue, and anything else you'd normally ask — so the details are waiting for you.
  • It alerts you instantly. Text and email the moment a job comes in, with everything attached.
  • It logs everything. Every call, booking, and conversation lands in your dashboard so nothing falls through the cracks.

What you do

  • Forward the calls you can't take. Keep your number. Forward after hours, when you're busy, or all the time — your call.
  • Keep your info current. Change your hours or add a service in the dashboard and the receptionist speaks from the new info immediately.
  • Check your alerts. That's the whole "monitoring" job.

4

See it working — in dollars, not "impressions"

Most marketing reports are a wall of charts that never answer the only question that matters: did this make me money? Ours are built backward from that question.

ReportingYour dashboard

What we do

  • Your own dashboard — every call, contact, job, and appointment in one place, branded to your business. Calls the receptionist took, jobs it booked, what came in overnight.
  • Monthly summary (Growth) — where you rank, what got posted, how your reviews grew, and how many calls came in.
  • Attribution loop (Super Growth) — calls → booked → closed. Which calls came from where, which ones turned into appointments, and which ones turned into paid jobs. Marketing spend on one side, revenue on the other.

What you do

  • Mark jobs done in the dashboard. That's what closes the loop from "booked" to "paid" — a tap when you finish the job.
  • Read the report. Five minutes a month. If a number's going the wrong way, we'll already be on it.
