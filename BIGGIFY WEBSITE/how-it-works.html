The problem

The business you don't see is the business you're losing

You never see the calls that go to voicemail — you only see the jobs that show up on the schedule. Here's what's happening in between.

Why calls get missed

  • You're on a ladder, under a sink, or elbow-deep in a job — the phone isn't an option.
  • It's 7 PM, a weekend, or a holiday, and the office is closed.
  • Two calls come in at once and only one gets answered.
  • You're driving between jobs and can't safely pick up.

What happens next

  • Most callers don't leave a voicemail — they just hang up.
  • The ones who do rarely get a callback fast enough to still want it.
  • They call the next name on the list, and that business books the job instead.
  • You never know it happened — there's no missed-job report to look at.
How Biggify works

What actually happens on a call

You don't need to know how the technology works — just what it does for your business.

1

It answers, every time

Calls you can't get to ring through to Biggify instead of voicemail. It picks up immediately, in a natural voice, and greets the caller using your business name, services, and hours.

2

It handles the conversation

It answers common questions about what you offer and your service area, figures out what the caller needs, and checks your real calendar for open times — all in one call, no hold music.

3

It books the job

If a time works, it books the appointment directly onto your calendar right then, while the customer is still on the phone — not a "someone will call you back."

4

It texts the customer

Right after the call, the customer gets a short text asking for the address, the issue, and anything else you'd normally ask — so the details are waiting for you.

5

It alerts you instantly

You get a text and email the moment a new job comes in, with everything attached — no need to sit by the phone or check in constantly.

6

It's all in your dashboard

Every call, every booking, every conversation is logged in your own dashboard, so nothing falls through the cracks and you have a record of every lead that came in.
