FAQ

Questions, answered

Something else? Call or text Wyatt at (941) 401-8288.

Do I have to take everything, or can I start with one service?

Start wherever your biggest leak is. Plenty of businesses begin with just the receptionist, or just Growth, and add on later. The plans are built so the pieces work on their own and work better together.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your existing number. You simply forward calls you can't answer to Biggify, so the calls you'd otherwise miss get caught — while you still take every call you can.

Will the receptionist sound like a robot?

No. Biggify uses a natural, professional AI voice that greets callers, answers common questions, and books the job like a great front-desk receptionist would. Hear it on the demo line, or try it on your own calls with the free 30-day trial.

Do I own my website and domain?

Yes. Your domain stays in your name and the site is yours. We build it, host it, and keep it updated while you're with us — but it's your business's asset, not ours.

How does the text survey work?

After a call, the customer receives a short text with a few quick questions so you have the address, the issue, and their preferred time before you follow up. Customers can reply STOP at any time to opt out.

What do the reports actually show me?

On Growth you get a monthly summary of your rankings, profile activity, and reviews. On Super Growth you get the full attribution loop — which calls came from where, which ones got booked, and which ones closed — so you can see marketing turn into money.

How fast can I get set up?

The receptionist is usually live within a few days. Website and marketing timelines depend on your plan and how much we're building — we'll give you a clear schedule in your quote.

What does it cost me to find out?

Nothing. We start with a free 15-minute call and a demo using your real business scenario, so you can see and hear it before you commit to anything.
