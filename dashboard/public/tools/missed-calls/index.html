    How does the missed call calculator work?

    It multiplies your own 3 numbers. Nothing else goes in.

    1. Missed calls a week × (how many book out of 10 ÷ 10) = the jobs you could be missing each week.
    2. Those jobs × your average job = what a week of missed calls could be worth.
    3. A week × 52 = a year. The year ÷ 12 = a month.

    Example with the sample numbers in the boxes: 5 missed calls a week, 5 out of 10 book, and a $400 job. 5 × 0.5 = 2.5 jobs a week. 2.5 × $400 = $1,000 a week. $1,000 × 52 = $52,000 a year. Your own numbers will give you your own answer.

    Is the number exact?

    No. It's an estimate. Some people do call back, so the real number may be lower. To make it real, write down every missed call for one week: the day, the time, and whether you got the job.
