    02

    Who's calling when you can't pick up

    Most missed calls aren't laziness. They're the job: you're under a sink, on a roof, driving, or it's 9 pm and you're eating dinner with your kids.

    • On a job. Hands full, and the caller with a leak won't wait for you to finish.
    • After hours and weekends. Evenings are when homeowners finally have time to call.
    • The price shoppers. Some people ring five companies at 11 pm to compare prices. You shouldn't have to talk to them at 11 pm. But one of them might be next week's best job, and you won't know unless someone takes their details.

    And many won't leave a voicemail. In a CallRail survey of 1,000 US consumers, only 42% said they leave one when a business doesn't answer, and 21% said they immediately call another business. That survey is from a company that sells phone software, so check it against your own call log.

    03

    What does an AI receptionist actually do on a call?

    An AI receptionist is a phone assistant that answers the calls you miss, takes the caller's details and texts them to you. Here's what mine does, start to finish. Ask any company you're looking at to show you the same.

    1. Your phone rings first.Pick up and nothing changes. It only answers the calls you miss or can't take.
    2. It answers as your answering service.It says it's an AI assistant. No pretending to be a person.
    3. It takes the details.The caller's name, number, what the job is, and the best time to call them back.
    4. It texts you right away.You get all of that in one text, so you can read it between jobs.
    5. You call back.You decide who's worth calling first and you win the job on the phone. It doesn't try to sell for you.
    The 11 pm price shopper, handled

    They get a polite answer and their details get written down. You read the text at breakfast and choose whether to call. Nobody wakes you up, and nobody gets voicemail.

    04

    When is an AI receptionist not worth it?

    ✓Worth it if

    • The calculator shows more than a job or two a month
    • You work alone or with a small crew and nobody answers while you're on a job
    • You get evening and weekend calls you can't take
    • You'll call people back the same day

    ✕Skip it if

    • Someone already answers every call live, all day
    • You rarely miss a call (your call log will tell you)
    • You won't call back quickly. It takes the message, it can't do the callback for you
    • Your callers are mostly people who hang up on any recording

    Cheaper fixes come first: a better voicemail greeting and a missed-call text-back. They're covered in Every missed call is a job your competitor answers.
