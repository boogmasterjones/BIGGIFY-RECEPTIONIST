Sales and scam calls aimed at trade owners, and what I do with each

Trade owners get the same calls over and over. "We can get you to page one of Google" is more spam than scam. "I'd like to invest in your business" is usually a sales call, not a check. And a call or text from "Google" asking for money? I assume it's not Google. Google says it never asks for payment info over the phone. My rules: give nothing private on a call you didn't make. Look them up yourself. Call back on a number you found.

01

My 3 rules for any call I didn't make

  1. Nothing private on the first call. No sales numbers, no bank info, no tax papers, no card details.
  2. Look them up yourself. Get the full company name. Find their real website on your own.
  3. Call back on a number you found, not the one they gave you.

That last one is the same advice Google gives for any call that seems off. Reach the company through a number or website you trust. Not the one in the message.

If it's real, it'll be real tomorrow

Must you decide now? Hang up.

02

"We can get you to page one of Google"

I get these calls all the time. I'd call them more spam than scam. They call a long list, and they don't know a thing about your shop.

The tell: they promise a spot. Google has its own page on hiring an SEO. It says no one can guarantee a number one ranking on Google. It also warns about firms that email you out of the blue. Or that claim a "special relationship" with Google.

What I do: I ask one thing. "Show me the calls you got a shop like mine." Not clicks. Calls. More on these calls.

03

"I'd like to invest in your business"

I get this call, and lots of contractors do too. It sounds great. But to me, it's usually a sales call, not a check.

What I do: I don't give out my sales, bank info or tax papers. I ask for the full company name and look them up myself. Then I call back on a number I find. More on these calls.

04

"This is Google. Pay us to fix your profile"

Someone calls, says they're Google, and wants money to fix your profile.

What I do: I hang up, and I don't press any buttons. My rule of thumb: if "Google" calls or texts me, I assume it's not Google. Google emails me, from a real Google address.

What Google says: it never asks for payment info over the phone. And it won't promise a special spot. Hang up on calls that seem off.

Then I log in to the profile myself and look. If it's really suspended, I use Google's own appeal form. And I never make a new profile. More on these calls.

05

Who gets these calls

Any business with a phone number on Google. That's every trade owner reading this. You're not being picked on. You're on a list.

The best defense is boring. Own your own stuff. Know where you log in. And never decide anything on a call you didn't make.

06

Quick answers

Does Google call businesses about their listing?

Google's help page says real Google calls can happen. But Google never asks for payment info over the phone. And it never promises a special spot. My own rule of thumb? If "Google" calls or texts me, I assume it's not Google. Google emails me from a real Google address.

Can anyone guarantee my business #1 on Google?

No. Google has a page on hiring an SEO. It says no one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google.

What should I never give out on a cold call?

My rule: on a call I didn't make, no sales, bank info or tax papers. And no card details to anyone saying they're Google.
