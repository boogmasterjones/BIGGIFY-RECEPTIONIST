Quick answers

What's the most important question to ask a marketing agency?

"If I leave, does all of it come with me?" Your website, domain, Google profile and phone number should be in your name, and you should be able to log in to each one yourself.

Can a marketing agency promise #1 on Google?

No one can promise that. Google picks, not them. Ask for real call numbers and real reviews instead.
