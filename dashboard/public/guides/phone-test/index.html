The 1-minute phone test: what your customers hear when they call

Grab a second phone and call your own business like a customer would. Let it ring all the way out and listen to what they hear. Then tap the call button on your Google profile and on your website, and make sure each one rings the right number. If a call rings out, a lot of people won't leave a message. They call the next shop.

What you need

  • A second phone. Your spouse's, a worker's, or a friend's.
  • One minute.
  • Your Google profile and your website open on that second phone.
02

The test

03

What to look for

  • It rang out with no answer. That's the big one. A lot of people won't leave a message. They call the next shop.
  • The voicemail is full, or it's a cell phone's default message. The caller can't tell they reached the right business.
  • A call button rings an old number. Fix that one first. Every call from that button goes to the wrong place.
  • There's no call button on the first screen of your site. People have to copy your number. A lot of them won't bother.
04

Do it after hours too

Call again at 7 at night, or on a Sunday. Some customers can only call after work.

If your Google profile says you're open, someone should pick up. If you show closed, some people will just call the next shop.

Quick answers

How do I test my business phone line?

Call it from another phone like a customer would. Count the rings, let it ring all the way out, and listen to what they hear. Then tap the call buttons on your Google profile and website to make sure they ring the right number.

What happens when a customer's call rings out?

A lot of people won't leave a message. They call the next shop.
