The client

A client of mine had 164 Google reviews. That's a lot. When I checked, they still didn't show up for their main search.

So more reviews weren't the fix. I looked at the profile instead. Here's what I found.

02

Problem 1: the website button

The website button on the profile went to a booking page. Not their own site.

A booking page doesn't say what they do, where they work or why they're good. Their own website does. That's where people go to check you out.

Check yours

Open your Google profile on your phone. Tap the website button. Does it open your site, on the right page?

03

Problem 2: the hours

The hours showed closed every night and on Sundays.

When you show closed, Google shows you less at those times. And a customer who looks you up at 7 at night sees "Closed". Some will just call the next shop.

Show the hours you really pick up the phone. If calls get answered at night, show that. If they don't, don't fake it.

04

What I take from it

  • Reviews aren't always the problem. 164 of them didn't help with these two holes.
  • Check the profile first when the review count looks fine but the phone is quiet.
  • Small things matter. A wrong link and the wrong hours can undo a lot of good work.
