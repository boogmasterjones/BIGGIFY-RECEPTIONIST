01

The only 3 numbers I care about

Most marketing reports are full of clicks and views. I only care about three numbers:

  1. Calls.
  2. Jobs booked from those calls.
  3. The money from those jobs.

Clicks don't pay for the truck.

02

An example

Say you get twenty calls in a month. You book eight jobs. They bring in six thousand dollars.

Those are the numbers I watch. Not how many people saw a post.

03

How I count them

  • Calls: my clients get their own line, so I count the calls.
  • Jobs and money: they log the jobs, so I see the money.

You can do it yourself with a notebook. Every call, did it become a job, and what did the job pay.

04

That's why my promise is simple: 300% ROI in 3 months or you don't pay.

I can only promise that because I'm watching the numbers that matter.
