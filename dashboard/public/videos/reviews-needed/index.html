How many reviews

How many Google reviews do you really need? I checked for my clients

01

My number

From what I've seen, it's about 30. That's when the phone starts to ring.

After that, the total matters less. What matters is how many new ones come in each week.

That's my own experience with my clients. It's not a Google rule. And 30 isn't a finish line. Small towns can need fewer. Giants need way more. Either way, I ask every customer.

02

How I find the bar in any town

  1. Search what a customer would type. The trade and the town. Like "plumber Venice FL".
  2. Look at the shops that show up first.
  3. Count their reviews. That's the bar you're up against.

Your town moves the bar. If the shops near you have ten reviews, you need fewer. If they have two thousand, that's a different plan.

03

Three of my clients, three answers

I won't name them. But here's what I found when I checked in October.

1. A design shop: low bar

They had zero reviews. The shops around them had 1, 7, 7 and 9.

So about ten reviews would put them ahead of nearly all of them. Last time I checked, they were up to three. Few shops, low bar.

2. A water heater guy: a fight I don't pick

The plumbers on his search had piles of reviews. Five hundred to over two thousand.

He had sixteen. Last time I checked, he had twenty-four. I'm not going to win that fight this year, so I don't pick it.

Instead, I aim him at one job in one town. Like drain line cleaning, in a town he serves. People already find him for searches like that.

3. A floor shop: reviews weren't the problem

They had 164 reviews. When I checked, they still didn't show up for their main search.

So I looked at the profile. The website button went to a booking page, not their own site. And it showed closed every night and on Sundays. When you show closed, Google shows you less at those times.

More reviews would not fix that. When reviews aren't the problem, I fix the profile first.

04

The takeaway

  • Find your bar. Search your trade and town. Count the top three.
  • Aim for about 30, then keep a steady pace each week.
  • If you're way behind a giant, pick a smaller fight: one job, one town.
  • Plenty of reviews and still no calls? Check the profile, not the review count.
