02

The 60-second domain check

Your domain is your web address, like yourshop.com. Start there.

  1. Go to lookup.icann.org.
  2. Type in your web address and look it up.
  3. Write down the registrar. That's the company where your domain lives.
  4. Write down the date it runs out.
  5. Look at the contact info. The owner's name is often hidden for privacy. That's normal, not a red flag.
  6. Now try to log in at that registrar yourself.

If you can't log in, you don't own it yet. Find out who can, while you're still on good terms.
