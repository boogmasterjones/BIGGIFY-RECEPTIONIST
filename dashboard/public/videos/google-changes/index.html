Google profile changes

How I change my clients' Google profiles without getting them suspended

I change my clients' Google profiles all the time. None of them has been suspended. My rules: change one big thing, then wait about a week. The big things are the name, address, main phone, main category and website. Use the real name only. To swap a phone, add the new one as main. Keep the old one as an extra. Show real hours. Never use a fake address. Keep one profile per shop.

01

Why owners are scared to touch Google

Lots of owners are scared to touch their Google profile. They read posts like "Google hid my shop after I changed my hours."

I change my clients' profiles all the time. None of my clients has been suspended. That's not luck. It's these rules.

First, know this. Google checks the edits you make. Google says most clear in a few minutes. Some can take up to thirty days.

02

Rule 1: one big change, then wait

Now think like a scammer. He takes over a profile. Then he changes the name, phone and address all at once.

And when I change a lot at once, Google often flags it. So I go slow.

My rule: I change one big thing, then I wait about a week. The big ones are:

  • The name
  • The address
  • The main phone
  • The main category
  • The website

Here's an example. Week one is the phone. Week two is the category. Photos, posts and service lists? Those I change any time.

03

Rule 2: the real name only

The name on Google is the real name. The one on the truck, the sign and the bills.

Google's rules say no extra words in the name. Stuff in a town or "best plumber", and you can get suspended.

04

Rule 3: the safe phone swap

  1. The new line goes in as the main number.
  2. The old number stays on as an extra number.
  3. The website gets the new number that same week.

Google also says the number must be in the shop's control. My clients' lines go with them if they ever leave me. There's a step-by-step on the phone swap page.

05

Rule 4: real hours only

Google asks for the hours you really serve people.

So I only show 24/7 if every call gets picked up, day or night. For my clients, my AI picks up when they can't.

Real hours help you get calls, too. If you really pick up after five, show it. Say someone looks you up at six and sees "closed". They call the next shop.

06

Rule 5: no fake address

No P.O. box, no mail drop, no office you rent but don't work from.

If they drive to their customers, I hide the home address. Then I set the towns they really serve. More on the fake address page.

07

Rule 6: one profile per shop

One profile per shop. Never a second one. Google only allows one per business.

08

What can still go wrong

Google wants to mail you a code

Say Google wants you to prove the shop is real, with a code by mail. Google says while you wait:

  • Don't touch the name, address or category.
  • Don't ask for a new code. A new one cancels the first.

An edit is waiting

An edit can sit there, waiting to be checked. My rule is I don't keep poking it. I wait.

The profile gets suspended

If a profile ever does get suspended, Google says don't make a new one. I'd fix the problem and use Google's appeal form.

Send proof, like a shop license or a power bill. Google says you only get one try. So I get it right the first time.

Someone calls "from Google" wanting money

I hang up. Google says it never asks for payment info over the phone.

My rule of thumb: a call or text from "Google"? I assume it's not Google. Google emails me, from a real Google address. More on the pay-to-fix page.

Quick answers

How long does Google take to check an edit to my profile?

Google says most edits are checked in a few minutes. But some can take up to 30 days.

Which Google profile changes can I make any time?

Photos, posts and service lists. I change those any time. The big ones I change one at a time, about a week apart. That's the name, address, main phone, main category and website.

What do I do if my Google profile is suspended?

Google says don't make a new profile. Fix the problem. Then use Google's appeal form. Send proof, like a business license or a utility bill. Google says you can only send an appeal once, so get it right.

Does Google call businesses asking for money?

Google says it never asks for payment info over the phone. My own rule of thumb? If "Google" calls or texts me, I assume it's not Google.

Your next steps

  1. Make a list of the changes you want. Mark the big ones. Name. Address. Main phone. Main category. Website.
  2. Do one big one this week. Photos, posts and services can go any time.
  3. Wait about a week before the next big one. Don't keep poking an edit that's waiting.
