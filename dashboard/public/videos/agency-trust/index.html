Choosing a marketer

How I make sure a marketing agency is trustworthy

Before I'd trust a marketing agency, I check five things. Who owns my stuff? My website, domain, Google profile and phone number should be in my name. Can they show real results? I mean calls and reviews, not "number one on Google." What happens if I leave? Do they tell me in plain words what they did each month? Do they stand behind it? I hold myself to the same rules. My clients own their own stuff.

Why I check these 5 things

A friend of mine got burned by a marketing guy. That's why I check these five things before I'd trust anyone with a business. And here's the exact question to ask for each one.

02

1. Who owns my stuff?

My site. My domain. My Google profile. My phone number. All of it should be in my name. Not theirs.

My phone number is how customers find me. If I leave, I want to take it all with me. I don't want to start over from zero.

Ask them

"If I leave, does all of it come with me?"

If they dodge it, I walk.

03

2. Can they show real results?

Real call numbers. Real reviews. Not "we'll get you number one on Google".

Nobody can promise number one. Google picks, not them. And the map changes with where you stand. Your spot at home is not your spot across town. So I ask for real calls, real reviews and real jobs. Those are what pay you.

Ask them

"How many calls did your clients get last month?"

A good agency knows.

04

3. What happens if I leave?

No lock-in. No fees to get my own stuff back.

Ask them

"If I quit next month, what does it cost me?"

The answer should be short.

05

4. Do they tell me what they did each month?

In plain words I get. If I can't tell what I'm paying for, that's a red flag.

Ask them

"What did you do for me last month?"

06

5. Do they stand behind it?

What happens if it doesn't work? Get it in writing.

Mine is simple. 300% ROI in 8 months, or your money back. Plus I work for free until you hit 300% ROI.

07

I hold myself to the same rules

My clients own their own stuff. If they ever leave, I hand over the site. And I move their number to them.

That's the "if I leave, does all of it come with me?" test. I want my answer to be yes.

08

Quick answers

What should I ask a marketing agency before I sign?

My five: Who owns my stuff? Can you show real results? What happens if I leave? Will you tell me what you did each month in plain words? Do you stand behind your work?

Should my marketing company own my website or Google profile?

No. Your website, domain, Google profile and phone number should be in your name. The test I use: "If I leave, does all of it come with me?"

Can a marketing company guarantee #1 on Google?

No one can promise that. Google picks, not them. Ask for real calls, real reviews and real jobs instead.
