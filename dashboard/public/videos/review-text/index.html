Review texts

The exact text I send to get Google reviews

My review ask is one short text, sent right after the job, from the business's own number, with a link that opens the review box in one tap. It names the customer, names the business, says thanks and asks for a quick review. If they don't leave one, I send two kind follow-ups a week apart, then stop.

01

The text, word for word

Here it is. Steal it. Swap in your customer's name and your business name. (Sarah and Sam's Plumbing are just examples.)

The review ask

"Hi Sarah, this is Sam's Plumbing, texting from our new business line. Thanks for having us out today! Would you leave us a quick Google review?"

Then paste your review link on the next line. That's the whole thing.

02

Why it works: 3 things

  1. It goes out right after the job. They just watched you fix it. That's when they feel it most.
  2. It comes from the business number. They know who it is. It doesn't look like a stranger or a scam.
  3. The link opens the review box in one tap. Not a search. Not your profile. The box where they type.

Miss any one of those and fewer people do it. A text sent a week later, from a number they don't know, with a link to a Google search? Most people won't bother.

03

If they don't leave one

I follow up twice, a week apart. Then I stop.

A week later

"Hi Sarah, it's Sam's Plumbing again. If you have a minute, a quick review would really help us."

The last one

"Last one from us, Sarah. No worries if not. Here's the link."

Then stop

Phone carriers want you to stop, and nobody likes a pest. If someone texts STOP, never text them again.

04

What not to add

  • No gift or discount for a review. I never pay for reviews.
  • No "if you're happy" line. I ask every customer, not just the happy ones.
  • No long story. Short and kind gets read. Long gets skipped.
05

Quick answers

Why say "texting from our new business line"?

If the number is new to them, it tells them who you are, so they don't think it's spam.

When should I send it?

Right after the job, while they're still happy with the work. The same day, not next week.

Know an owner this would help?

Send it to them.
