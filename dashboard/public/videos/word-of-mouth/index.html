01

What happens 10 seconds after a friend says your name

"I get all my work from word of mouth." I hear that on my calls all the time. I love it. Word of mouth is the best kind of work.

But here's what happens right after a friend says your name. Say Dave tells his neighbor about you. She grabs her phone and looks you up.

She sees your reviews, your photos and your site. Then she calls you. Or the next one.

02

Where the tip gets lost

Here's an example. Say she finds eight reviews. The last one is two years old.

Right under you, there's a shop with a hundred and fifty. One is from last week. Who would you call?

Or she finds no website. Or she calls, and it goes to voicemail. The tip was real. The job still went to someone else.

03

I don't replace word of mouth. I make it louder

Here's how I do it for my trade businesses:

  1. I ask every customer for a review. I made a whole page on how I get them.
  2. I fill in the whole Google profile. Each service, the towns they serve, and real photos from real jobs.
  3. A website that says the job and the town. So she knows she found the right shop.
  4. Every call gets picked up. The line rings the owner first. If he can't get it, my receptionist does.
04

A review is word of mouth that strangers can read

When a friend tells a friend, one person hears it. When they write a review, everyone who looks you up can read it.

So I don't just ask the happy ones. I ask them all, right after the job, from the shop's own number. And I hand them a QR card so it's easy to do later.

05

The test I do on every new client

I look up their name on my phone, like a stranger would. I use a private tab, so I see what a stranger sees.

Then I look at four things:

  • Reviews: how many, and how new?
  • Photos: are they real jobs?
  • Website: does it work, and does it say the job and the town?
  • Phone: does it get picked up?

Miss one, and a referral can slip away. There's a full walk-through on the name test page.

06

Quick answers

If I get all my work from word of mouth, do I need marketing?

I don't try to replace word of mouth. I make it louder. People still look you up before they call. So your reviews, Google profile, website and phone must back up what their friend said.

Why are reviews called word of mouth?

A review is word of mouth that strangers can read. When a friend tells a friend, one person hears it. A review can be read by everyone who looks you up.
