The name test

Look up your own business name: the 4 things I look for

On every new client, I look up their business name on my phone. I use a private tab, so I see what a stranger sees. Then I check four things. How many reviews, and how new? Real photos? A website that works? A phone that gets picked up? Miss one, and a referral can slip away.

01

How I do it

  1. Open a private tab on your phone. (In most phone browsers it's called private or incognito.) That way you see closer to what a stranger sees.
  2. Search your business name. Just the name, like a friend would tell someone.
  3. Look at what comes up, top to bottom, before you tap anything.

I do this on every new client. On camera, I show it with my own name, Biggify.

The 4 things I look for

The name test0 of 4 done
All done. Nice work.

Miss one, and a referral can slip away. Their friend said your name. Now the screen has to back it up.

03

What each miss tells me

  • Few or old reviews: start asking every customer, this week. A steady pace beats a burst.
  • No real photos: take three on the next job. Before, after, and the work up close.
  • No site, or a broken button: the people checking you out have nowhere to go.
  • The call rings out: a lot of people won't leave a message. They call the next shop.

Your own phone knows you. A private tab shows you closer to what a stranger sees. It leaves out your own history and sign-in.
