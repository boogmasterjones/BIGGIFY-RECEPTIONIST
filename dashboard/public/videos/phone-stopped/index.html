The phone stopped ringing? These are the 5 things I check first

When an owner tells me the phone stopped ringing, I check five things on the Google profile first: is the main category his exact trade, are his services and towns there, when was the last review, does the phone number on the profile ring, and does the website button go to his site. All five take a few minutes on a phone.

Why these five first

  • The category tells Google what you are. A broad one can mean you don't show for the exact job people search.
  • Services and towns tell Google what you do and where. If they're missing, you're harder to match.
  • The last review is what customers look at. An old one makes a shop look closed.
  • The phone and the website button are how people reach you. If either one is broken, the calls stop no matter what else is right.
One big change at a time

If you find a problem with the category, phone or website, fix one, then wait about a week before the next big one. See how I change Google profiles safely.
