"Page one" calls

"We can get you to page one of Google." What I think of these calls

01

More spam than scam

"We can get you to page one of Google." I get these calls all the time. I bet you do too.

I'd call it more spam than scam. They call a long list of businesses. They don't know a thing about your shop. They didn't look at your profile or your site.

02

The tell: the promise

They promise a spot on Google. That's the tell.

Google has its own page on hiring an SEO. It says no one can guarantee a number one ranking on Google. It also warns about firms that email you out of the blue. Or that claim a "special relationship" with Google.

Is the first thing they sell you a spot? Then they're selling something nobody can promise.

03

The one question I ask

Ask them this

"Show me the calls you got a shop like mine."

Not clicks. Not "impressions". Not a spot on the map. Calls.

A good marketing company can answer that. A list-dialer can't.

Same for any marketer

Before you sign, ask who owns your stuff. Ask what happens if you leave. Ask how they'll show you real results. It's all on my free agency checklist.

04

Quick answers

Can anyone guarantee my business a #1 spot on Google?

No. Google has a page on hiring an SEO. It says no one can guarantee a #1 ranking on Google.

Are SEO cold calls a scam?

To me, most are more spam than scam. They call a long list and don't know your shop. Ask them for the calls they got a shop like yours. If they can't show you, move on.
