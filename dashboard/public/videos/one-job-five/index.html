How I turn one finished job into 5 pieces of marketing

I ask my trade shops for three photos on every job: before, after, and the work up close. They send them to me through one link. That one job then goes five places: a blog post on the website, a Google post and new profile photos, a short story on Facebook, a photo-first post on Instagram, and anywhere else the owner posts, like Nextdoor. Each place gets its own words. Real photos only, never AI.

01

It starts with three photos

You finish a job. You pack up and drive to the next one. I make that one job work five more times.

It starts with photos. I ask for three on every job:

The 3 photos0 of 3 done
All done. Nice work.
  • Sharp, not blurry.
  • The whole job in the shot.
  • No kids in the photo, unless the owner says OK.

They send them to me through one link. That's all the crew has to do.

02

The 5 places one job goes

Here's an example. Say it was a water heater swap in a town they serve.

  1. A blog post on the website. It says what the crew did and where, with the photos. It's one more page Google can find for that job and that town.
  2. Google. The after photo goes up as a Google post. And the best photos go on the Google profile too.
  3. Facebook. Same job, but told like a short story. What was wrong, what they did, how it looks now.
  4. Instagram. Here the photo does the talking. Best shot first, and just a few words.
  5. Anywhere else you post. Say you're on Nextdoor. Same photos, a few new words.
03

New words for each place

I don't paste the same words in each place. Each one gets words that fit it.

  • The blog explains the job and the town.
  • Google gets short and clear.
  • Facebook gets the little story.
  • Instagram lets the photo lead.
04

What Facebook and Instagram are for

Will Facebook and Instagram ring your phone? Sometimes. But that's not why I post there.

They help Google and AI like you more. And when someone checks you out, they see real, recent work.

05

Real photos, never AI

Why real photos, and never AI pictures? People can tell. And a fake picture makes a promise you can't keep. More on the real photos page.

That's one job, five times. And it all starts with three photos on a phone.
