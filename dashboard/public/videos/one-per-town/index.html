Why I only take one plumber per town

I only take one client per trade per town, and no more than four of the same trade in a county. The reason is simple: I can't push two plumbers up the same Google search. Two plumbers, one search, who would I help win? So if I work with you, I'm only in your corner. It's the same for every trade.

01

My rule

  • One client per trade, per town.
  • No more than four of the same trade in a county.

So never two plumbers in one town. And never five plumbers in one county. Plumber is just the example. It's the same for every trade.

02

Why

Because I can't push two plumbers up the same Google search.

Think about it. Two plumbers, one search. Who would I help win? I couldn't give either one my best.

So if I work with you, I'm only in your corner.
