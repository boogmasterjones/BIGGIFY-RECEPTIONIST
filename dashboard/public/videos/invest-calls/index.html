"Investor" calls

"I'd like to invest in your business." What I do with these calls

"I'd like to invest in your business" sounds great. But to me, it's usually a sales call, not a check. My rule: on a first call, I don't give out my sales. Or my bank info or tax papers. I ask for the full company name. I look them up myself. Then I call back on a number I find. If it's real, it will still be real tomorrow.

01

The call

"Hi, I'd like to invest in your business."

I get this call. Lots of contractors do too. It sounds great. Someone wants to give you money?

But to me, it's usually a sales call, not a check.

02

My rule for a first call

On a first call, I don't give out:

  • My sales numbers
  • My bank info
  • My tax papers

Not to anyone, on a call I didn't make.

03

How I check who it is

  1. Ask for the full company name. Write it down.
  2. Say you'll call back. Then hang up.
  3. Look them up yourself. Search the name. Find their real website.
  4. Call back on a number you found, not the one they gave you.

That last step is the same advice Google gives for any call that seems off. Reach the company with a number or website you trust. Not the one in the message.

Real tomorrow too

If it's real, it will still be real tomorrow. Do they say you have to decide on this call? That's a reason to hang up.

04

Quick answers

Are "invest in your business" calls a scam?

I can't say who every caller is. To me, it's usually a sales call, not a check. Either way, I don't share sales, bank info or tax papers on a first call. And I call back on a number I found myself.
