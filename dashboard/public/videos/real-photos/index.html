01

Stock photos make you look like everyone else

Stock photos make you look like everyone else. Customers can tell. The smiling model in a hard hat is on a hundred other sites.

So I use real photos instead. The truck, the crew, and the job when it's done.

02

Why I never use AI pictures

I never use AI pictures for my trade shops. Not even once.

  1. People can tell. Something looks a bit off, and they stop trusting the rest.
  2. A fake picture makes a promise. It shows work you didn't do. Then the real job has to live up to it.
  3. If it isn't their job, it doesn't go on. That's my rule.
03

Quick answers

Are phone photos good enough for my website?

Yes. Real beats pretty. Keep them sharp, not blurry, with the whole job in the shot.

Can I use AI pictures on my business website?

I never do. People can tell something looks off, and a fake picture shows work you didn't do. Then the real job has to live up to it.
