Two of my clients' website forms were broken: the 1-minute test that catches it

Two of my clients' quote forms broke. People filled them out and nothing came through, and those leads were gone. Now I use a one-minute test: open the site on a phone, tap the call button and make sure it rings, then send the form with the word "test" and check that the lead showed up where it should. Do it every week, because forms can break and nobody tells you.

Do it every week

Forms can break, and nobody tells you. So do this each week, on your own site.

Only test your own site

04

While you're on your phone

  • Can you see the call button without scrolling? On my sites, it sits on the first screen.
  • Does the first screen say your trade and your town? If not, people don't know they found the right shop.
05

Quick answers

How do I know if my website contact form is working?

Send it yourself with the word "test" and check that it shows up where it should, like your email or your phone. Do it every week.

Why would a website form stop working?

Forms can break when something else on the site or behind it changes. Nobody gets an alert unless someone checks. That's why I test them.
