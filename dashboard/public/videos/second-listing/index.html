A client's Google reviews were on a second listing: how I check for one

A client had real Google reviews, but his profile showed none. They were on a second listing nobody had claimed, with his old address and old phone number. Now I check every client for this: search the business name on Google Maps, then the phone number, then any old address. If a second listing turns up, it gets merged with the real one once Google has verified the real profile.

What happens next

For the first client, the fix isn't done yet. Here's the order:

  1. Google has to verify his real profile first.
  2. Then I ask Google to merge them.

Google changes its steps for this now and then. So check Google's own help pages for duplicate listings before you start.

Don't make a third one

If you find a second listing, don't make yet another profile to "start fresh". That only adds to the mess.

04

Quick answers

Why do I have two Google listings?

I don't always know the cause. Old info, like an old address or old phone number, is often how a second listing starts.

Can I get my reviews moved to my real profile?

Google can merge duplicate listings. In my client's case, Google has to verify his real profile first. Then I ask Google to merge them. Check Google's current help page on duplicate listings for the steps.
