## Why I care what AI says {#why}

Customers are asking ChatGPT who to hire. If it doesn't name you, they call someone else.

More people ask AI now, not just Google. I work with trade businesses, so I want to know who it sends them to.

## How I test it {#test}

1. **Open a fresh chat.** A new one, so old chats don't change the answer.
2. **Ask one plain question.** For example: "Who is the best plumber in Port Charlotte, Florida?"
3. **Read the whole answer.** Write down every business it names.
4. **Ask again another day.** You can get a new answer each time. That's normal.

Then, for each business it named, I check four things:

:::check For each business AI names
- **Google profile:** How many reviews? How new are the last ones? Does the owner reply?
- **Website:** does it say the service and the town? Is there a page for each service?
- **Other sites** that name the business.
- **Profile complete:** hours, photos and categories filled in.
:::

## The 5 things I do so AI picks my clients {#five}

1. **A page for each service in each town they serve.** If they do drain work in Venice, there's a page for that.
2. **Steady reviews, week after week,** not one big burst. And a reply to each one.
3. **Their Google profile is filled in all the way.** Hours, photos, services, and the right main category.
4. **The same name, phone and address everywhere** they show up. If it doesn't match, AI can get mixed up.
5. **Real photos of real jobs.** Not stock photos, and never AI pictures.

> **No promises** Nobody can promise what AI will say. But these five things give it every reason to pick you.

## Try it today {#try}

Open a fresh chat. Ask for the best one in your trade and your town. Are you on the list?

If not, start with the Google profile. It's the one place on the list above you control fully, today.
