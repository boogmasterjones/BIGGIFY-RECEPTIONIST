## More spam than scam {#spam}

"We can get you to page one of Google." I get these calls all the time. I bet you do too.

I'd call it more spam than scam. They call a long list of businesses. They don't know a thing about your shop. They didn't look at your profile or your site.

## The tell: the promise {#tell}

They promise a spot on Google. That's the tell.

Google has [its own page](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/do-i-need-seo) on hiring an SEO. It says no one can guarantee a number one ranking on Google. It also warns about firms that email you out of the blue. Or that claim a "special relationship" with Google.

Is the first thing they sell you a spot? Then they're selling something nobody can promise.

## The one question I ask {#question}

:::text Ask them this
"Show me the calls you got a shop like mine."
:::

Not clicks. Not "impressions". Not a spot on the map. Calls.

A good marketing company can answer that. A list-dialer can't.
