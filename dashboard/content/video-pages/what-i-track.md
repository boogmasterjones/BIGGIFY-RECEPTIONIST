## Why I don't chase the map spot {#map}

Lots of owners check where they show on the map. Some marketing reports are built on it.

I don't care where you show on the map. Here's why. Your spot changes with where you stand. Move a mile, and you can get a new list.

So a screenshot of "you're number 3" only tells you what one person saw, in one spot, at one time. It's not where your customers are standing.

## What I track instead {#track}

I track two things from the Google profile:

1. **Calls.** People who tapped call.
2. **Website clicks.** People who tapped through to the site.

Say they go from 20 a month to 30. That's real work coming in. That's the number I watch.

## Find your own numbers {#find}

You can see yours right now:

1. **Sign in** to the Google account that manages your profile.
2. **Open your profile.** Search your business name, or open Google Maps.
3. **Find the part that shows how people found you,** with calls and website clicks.
4. **Write down last month.** Check again next month.

> **When someone shows you a map ranking** Ask them where they were standing. Then ask for calls instead. Calls are what pay you.
