## How to use it {#use}

Print this. Take it to your next meeting. Tick a box for every good answer. It remembers your ticks on this device.

The big test is one question. If you only ask one, ask this:

:::text The one question
"If I leave, does all of it come with me?"
:::

## 1. Who owns my stuff? {#own}

:::check Who owns my stuff
- My **website** is in my name.
- My **domain** (my web address) is in my name.
- My **Google profile** is in my name. They are a manager, not the owner.
- My **phone number** is in my name.
- I can log in to each one myself, today.
:::

>! **Red flag** "Don't worry, we handle all that."

## 2. Can you show me real results? {#results}

:::check Real results
- They show real call numbers.
- They show real reviews.
- They don't promise "number one on Google". No one can promise that.
:::

Ask them: "How many calls did your clients get last month?" A good agency knows.

>! **Red flag** Big promises and no proof.

## 3. What happens if I leave? {#leave}

:::check If I leave
- No lock-in.
- No fees to get my own stuff back.
- They will hand over my site and my number.
:::

Ask them: "If I quit next month, what does it cost me?" The answer should be short.

>! **Red flag** "You can leave, but the site stays with us."

## 4. Will you tell me what you did each month, in plain words? {#monthly}

:::check Each month
- I get a short note each month.
- It says what they did and what came of it.
- I can tell what I'm paying for.
:::

Ask them: "What did you do for me last month?"

>! **Red flag** Big words and no straight answer.

## 5. Do you stand behind your work? {#guarantee}

:::check Standing behind it
- They tell me what happens if it doesn't work.
- It's in writing.
:::

Mine is simple: **300% ROI in 3 months, or I work for free until you get it.** Or prepay 6 months: if I don't get you 300% ROI by then, you get all your money back, and I work for free until you do.

>! **Red flag** "There are no promises in marketing" and nothing else.
