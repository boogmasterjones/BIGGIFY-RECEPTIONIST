## The fight I don't pick {#dont}

One of my clients fixes water heaters. The plumbers on his search had five hundred to over two thousand reviews.

He had sixteen. I'm not going to win that fight this year. So I don't pick it.

Chasing two thousand reviews would take years. And the phone needs to ring now.

## The fight I pick {#pick}

I aim him at one job in one town. Like drain line cleaning, in a town he serves.

People already find him for searches like that. The big shops are built for the big search. They aren't always strong on the smaller ones.

Here's how I pick the fight:

1. **One job.** A job the shop wants more of.
2. **One town.** A town they really drive to. Never a fake address.
3. **One page.** A page on their website about that job in that town.

## Keep asking for reviews anyway {#keep}

Picking a smaller fight doesn't mean you stop asking. I still ask every customer for a review, on every job.

The reviews keep stacking up in the background. The one-job, one-town page brings calls while they do.

> **Then add the next one** When the first job-and-town is working, pick the next one. Same idea, one page at a time.
