## My answer: yes {#yes}

Do you need a website if you have a Google profile? I think you do. Here's why.

Your Google profile gets you found. But there's a website button on it. That's where people go to check you out.

If that button goes nowhere, some people just call the next guy. If it goes to a good site, you look like the real deal.

## What goes on my clients' sites {#what}

- **A page for each job** they do.
- **A page for each town** they serve.
- **A quote form,** up high on the page.
- **Real photos** of real jobs.
- **Their reviews,** word for word.

Each page gives Google, and customers, one more clear answer: this shop does this job, in this town.

## Where Facebook and Instagram fit {#social}

Facebook and Instagram help Google and AI like you. And they give people one more place to check you out.

Sometimes they bring a job. But mostly, they build trust. So I treat them as proof you're real and busy, not as the main way the phone rings.

> **Try it now** Open your own Google profile. Tap the website button. Where does it go? If it's a booking page, an old site or nothing at all, you just found your first fix.
