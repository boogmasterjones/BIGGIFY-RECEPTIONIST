## What the card is {#card}

It's a small card, like a business card. It has a QR code on it.

The customer points their phone camera at the code. It opens right to your Google review box. No searching. No hunting for a button.

## Why it works so well {#why}

It gets a lot more people to leave a review. Here's why:

- **A text gets buried.** A card doesn't. It sits on the counter or the fridge.
- **It's there when they have a minute.** Most people don't write a review on the spot. They do it later, when they sit down.
- **It's from you, in person.** They just met you. That counts.

And it costs a few cents a card.

## How my clients hand it out {#hand-out}

1. **Finish the job.** Make sure they're happy with the work.
2. **Hand over the card and say thanks.** Something like: "Thanks for having us. If you have a minute later, this goes right to our reviews."
3. **Don't stand over them.** If they don't scan it now, that's fine. It'll be there later.

They still get the review text too. The card and the text work together.

> **Every customer gets one** Not just the ones who seem happy. My clients hand one out on every job.

## What goes on the card {#what}

My free kit makes the card for you. Here's what's on it:

- **Your business name,** at the top.
- **A short ask:** "How did we do?"
- **One line on how:** An honest review really helps. Point your phone camera at the code. It takes about a minute.
- **The QR code,** big enough to scan from arm's length.
- **A thank-you** for choosing a local business.

You put in your name and your review link. It makes the card and a print sheet with 10 cards on one page.
