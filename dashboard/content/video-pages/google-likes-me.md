## Google tells you how it picks {#rules}

Google tells you, in writing, how it picks who shows up. It's on [Google's own help page about local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091). Most contractors have never read it.

It looks at three things. In plain words:

1. **Relevance:** do you do what they searched for?
2. **Distance:** are you close to them, or to the town they typed?
3. **Prominence:** do people know you? Google looks at your reviews, and what the rest of the web says about you.

Here's what I do about each one.

:::

## Relevance: say what you do {#relevance}

If they search "drain cleaning", do you say you do drain cleaning?

:::check Relevance
- The main category is your exact trade. Say you're a plumber, but it says builder. You're telling Google the wrong thing.
- Every service you do is listed on the profile.
- The towns you really serve are set as service areas.
- Your website has a page for each service, in each town you serve. One job, one town, one page.
:::

## Distance: don't cheat it {#distance}

Distance is the one you can't really change. So I don't try to cheat it.

- I set real service areas. Only towns they drive to.
- I only make town pages for towns they really serve.
- I never use a fake address. Ever. That can get a profile suspended.

More on that on the [fake address page](/videos/fake-address).

## Prominence: get known {#prominence}

1. **Reviews first.** Steady reviews, every week, and a reply to each one. There's a whole page on [how I get them](/videos/reviews-most).
2. **A Google post every week.**
3. **Real photos from real jobs.** Never AI pictures.
4. **The same name, phone and address everywhere.** When it all matches, nothing gets mixed up.

None of this is a quick trick. It builds slowly at first. Then it stacks up, week after week.

## How I know it's working {#tracking}

Here's the thing most owners check that I don't: where they show on the map.

That spot changes with where you stand. Move a mile, and you can get a new list. So it can't tell you much.

I track calls and website clicks from Google. Say they go from 20 a month to 30. That's the number I watch. You can see yours on your own Google profile. Look in the part that shows calls and clicks.

> **No promises** Nobody can promise you a spot on Google. These are the things Google says it looks at, and what I do about each.
