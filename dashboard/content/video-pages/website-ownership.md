## Your website might not be yours {#why}

You paid for it. But is it in your name? Some owners only find out when they try to leave.

Here's how I check, fast.

## The 60-second domain check {#check}

Your domain is your web address, like yourshop.com. Start there.

1. **Go to [lookup.icann.org](https://lookup.icann.org).**
2. **Type in your web address** and look it up.
3. **Write down the registrar.** That's the company where your domain lives.
4. **Write down the date it runs out.**
5. **Look at the contact info.** The owner's name is often hidden for privacy. That's normal, not a red flag.
6. **Now try to log in at that registrar yourself.**

If you can't log in, you don't own it yet. Find out who can, while you're still on good terms.

## The 4 keys {#keys}

Your domain is just one of 4 keys every business should own:

:::check The 4 keys
- Your **domain** (your web address)
- Your **website**
- Your **Google profile**
- Your **phone number**
:::

Do the same test for each one: can you log in yourself, today?

## My rule {#rule}

My clients own their own stuff. That's a rule I hold myself to. If a client ever leaves, I hand over the site. And I move their number to them.
