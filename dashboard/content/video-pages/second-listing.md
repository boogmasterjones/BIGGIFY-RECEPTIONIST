## What I found {#found}

A client had real Google reviews. His profile showed none.

They were on a second listing nobody had claimed. It had his old address and his old phone number. No website.

Old info like that is often how a second listing starts.

## The 1-minute check {#check}

:::check Second listing check
- Search the **business name** on Google Maps.
- Search the **phone number**, and any old numbers.
- Search any **old address** you used to work from.
- Write down any listing that isn't your real, claimed profile.
:::

Another client of mine had one too. So now I check every client for this.

## What happens next {#next}

For the first client, the fix isn't done yet. Here's the order:

1. **Google has to verify his real profile first.**
2. **Then I ask Google to merge them.**

Google changes its steps for this now and then. So check [Google's own help pages](https://support.google.com/business/) for duplicate listings before you start.

> **Don't make a third one** If you find a second listing, don't make yet another profile to "start fresh". That only adds to the mess.
