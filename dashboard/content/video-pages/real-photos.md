## Stock photos make you look like everyone else {#stock}

Stock photos make you look like everyone else. Customers can tell. The smiling model in a hard hat is on a hundred other sites.

So I use real photos instead. The truck, the crew, and the job when it's done.

## Why I never use AI pictures {#ai}

I never use AI pictures for my trade shops. Not even once.

1. **People can tell.** Something looks a bit off, and they stop trusting the rest.
2. **A fake picture makes a promise.** It shows work you didn't do. Then the real job has to live up to it.
3. **If it isn't their job, it doesn't go on.** That's my rule.

## Your phone photos are fine {#phone}

Real beats pretty. You don't need a photographer. You need a phone and ten seconds.

:::check Before you leave the next job
- The **truck**, parked at the job.
- The **crew**, if they're OK with it.
- The **finished work**, the whole job in the shot.
- Sharp, not blurry. Wipe the lens first.
:::

> **People in photos** Only put a person in a photo if they're OK with it. And no kids in job photos unless the owner says it's fine.

## Where the photos go {#where}

The same three photos can go on your website, your Google profile, Facebook and Instagram. See [how I turn one job into five pieces of marketing](/videos/one-job-five).
