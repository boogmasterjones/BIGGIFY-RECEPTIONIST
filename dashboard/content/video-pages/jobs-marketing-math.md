## The math {#math}

Here's an example. These are made-up numbers, just to show the math.

1. **Say marketing costs you $1,000 a month.**
2. **Say a job leaves you $500** after costs.
3. **$1,000 ÷ $500 = 2.** Two jobs pay for it.

Every job after that is money in your pocket.

## Now do it with your numbers {#yours}

1. **Grab your last invoice.** What did that job leave you, after materials, fuel and labor?
2. **Take what you pay for marketing each month.**
3. **Divide the marketing cost by what one job leaves you.** That's your break-even number of jobs.

If your marketing brings you more jobs than that, it's paying for itself.

## Cheap isn't the question {#cheap}

So I don't ask if marketing is cheap. I ask how many jobs it brings back.

Cheap marketing that brings no jobs costs more than good marketing that brings ten.
