## What a missed call really means {#missed-call}

You're on a roof. You're under a sink. You're driving. The phone rings, and you can't get it.

That's not laziness. It's the job. But the customer doesn't wait. A lot of people don't leave a message. They just call the next contractor on the list.

Honestly, if my pipe burst, I'd call the next one too.

> **The part that hurts** You never find out about that job. It doesn't show up anywhere. The caller just picks someone else.

## Say it happens all year {#all-year}

Here's an example. Say you miss a few calls a week. Say only one of those a week was a real job.

That's about 50 jobs a year that went to someone else. And you'd never know which ones.

Your numbers will be different. That's why I made a free calculator. You put in your own calls, and it does the math.

## How I make sure my trade businesses never miss a call {#how}

My clients get a business line. Here's what happens when it rings:

1. **The owner's phone rings first.** If he can take it, he presses 1 and talks. Same as any call.
2. **If he can't, my AI receptionist answers.** It gets the caller's name, their number and what they need.
3. **The owner gets a text** with all of that. He calls back when he's off the roof.

The customer gets an answer. The owner gets the text. Nobody calls the next guy.
