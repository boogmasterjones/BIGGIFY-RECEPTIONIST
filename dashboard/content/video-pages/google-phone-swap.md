## The swap, step by step {#steps}

1. **The new line goes in as the main number.** That's the one Google shows first.
2. **The old number stays on as an extra number.** Google allows extra numbers. People who saved the old one can still reach you.
3. **The website gets the new number that same week.** So Google sees the same number in both places.
4. **Nothing else big changes that week.** No name, address, category or website change.

## Why one big change at a time {#why}

A scammer who takes over a profile changes a lot at once. And when I change a lot at once, Google often flags it.

So a new phone number is that week's one big change. Photos and posts can still go up any time.

> **Same number everywhere** After the swap, put the new number everywhere people see you: the website, the truck, your cards, Facebook. When it all matches, nothing gets mixed up.

## Make sure the number is yours {#yours}

[Google's rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) say the phone number must be under the business's direct control.

My clients' lines go with them if they ever leave me. If someone else set up your number, ask them in writing: "If I leave, does my number come with me?"
