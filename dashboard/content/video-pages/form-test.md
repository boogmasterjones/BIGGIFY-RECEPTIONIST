## What happened {#happened}

Two of my clients' website forms were broken. People filled them out, and nothing came through. Those leads were just gone.

Nobody knew until I tested them. That's the scary part. A broken form doesn't tell you it's broken.

## The 1-minute test {#test}

:::check The 1-minute website test
- Open your site **on your phone**.
- **Tap the call button.** Does it ring your business?
- Fill in the quote form with the word **test** and send it.
- Check that the lead **showed up where it should** (your email, your phone or your system).
:::

## Do it every week {#weekly}

Forms can break, and nobody tells you. So do this each week, on your own site.

Put a reminder in your phone. Same day each week. One minute.

> **Only test your own site** Never send a fake lead to another business. Their time is real.

## While you're on your phone {#also}

- **Can you see the call button without scrolling?** On my sites, it sits on the first screen.
- **Does the first screen say your trade and your town?** If not, people don't know they found the right shop.
