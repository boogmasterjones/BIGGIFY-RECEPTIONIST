## Question 1 {#when}

:::text Ask before you leave
"When are you hoping to get this done?"
:::

Now you have a date. On that day, you check in.

It's not pushy, because they told you to. When the day comes, your text is easy:

:::text On their date
"Hi, you said you hoped to start around now. Any questions I can help with?"
:::

## Question 2 {#who}

:::text Ask before you leave
"Who else needs to see this?"
:::

A wife, a husband, a landlord. That's often why a job gets stuck. The person you met says yes. Then someone you never met says "let's wait".

So offer to answer their questions too. A quick call, or a text they can read. Now you're not waiting on someone you never met.

## Why these two {#why}

- **The first one** turns "I'll think about it" into a date.
- **The second one** finds the real decision.

Ask both before you leave the door. Then send the estimate the same day.
