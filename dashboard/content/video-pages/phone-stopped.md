## The 5 checks {#checks}

:::check Phone stopped ringing
- **Main category:** is it your exact trade? Or too broad, like just "contractor"?
- **Services and towns:** are all your services listed? And the towns you serve?
- **Last review:** when was the newest one? Months ago looks quiet.
- **The phone:** call the number on the profile. Does it ring?
- **The website link:** tap it. Does it go to your site?
:::

You can do all five tonight, on your phone. It takes a few minutes.

## Why these five first {#why}

- **The category** tells Google what you are. A broad one can mean you don't show for the exact job people search.
- **Services and towns** tell Google what you do and where. If they're missing, you're harder to match.
- **The last review** is what customers look at. An old one makes a shop look closed.
- **The phone and the website button** are how people reach you. If either one is broken, the calls stop no matter what else is right.

> **One big change at a time** If you find a problem with the category, phone or website, fix one, then wait about a week before the next big one. See [how I change Google profiles safely](/videos/google-changes).
