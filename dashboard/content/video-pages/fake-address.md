## The rule I never break {#rule}

I never put a fake address on Google. Not even one town over, where the jobs are.

It's tempting. The bigger town is right there. But it breaks Google's rules. And Google can shut down your whole listing. Then you have nothing.

## What counts as fake {#fake}

Google's own [rules for business profiles](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177) say:

- **No P.O. boxes.**
- **No mailboxes at remote places.**
- **No rented address where you don't really work.** An office you rent but don't work from isn't eligible.

## The honest way to reach the next town {#honest}

1. **If they work from home, I hide the address.** Google says service-area businesses should do this.
2. **I set real service areas.** Only towns they really drive to.
3. **I build a website page for each town they serve.** That's the honest way to show up in the next town over.

> **Real towns only** I only make town pages for towns a client really serves. If they won't drive there, it doesn't get a page.

## Why it's worth doing the slow way {#why}

A fake address puts the whole listing at risk, reviews and all.

Service areas and town pages take longer. But nobody can take them away from you.
